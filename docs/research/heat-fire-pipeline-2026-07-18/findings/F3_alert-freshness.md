# F3: Nearby heat alerts needed observation-age controls

Status: confirmed and remediated.

The prior background logic warned for every point within five kilometres from a 24-hour feed and did not mention observation age. The remediation normalizes the provider timestamp, limits nearby warnings to observations no older than six hours, groups multiple pixels into one warning, and states that the result is not a confirmed structure fire.

Evidence: sources 02, 03, and 06.
